Our family heritage

Four generations of olive tradition.

Our family olive story began with our great-grandfather, who planted the first olive trees. Our grandfather continued the tradition and planted more than 100 additional trees. In 2015, our father planted more than 70 new trees, and in 2024 the next generation continued the work by planting about 52 more.

Today, our family olive groves cover approximately 14 hectares in the Chraaf and Chorbane area of Tunisia. These trees are more than a source of olive oil — they represent our family's heritage, generations of work and a lasting connection to the land.
